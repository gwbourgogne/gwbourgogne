# 🎒 gwbourgogne

Welcome to **gwbourgogne**, an online school supplies store created to provide students, parents, teachers, and schools with a wide variety of practical, affordable, and high-quality educational products.

Our goal is to make shopping for school essentials simple, organized, and convenient by offering everything needed for classrooms, homework, creative projects, and everyday learning in one place.

## 🏪 About gwbourgogne

**gwbourgogne** is a modern online store focused on school supplies, stationery, classroom essentials, and educational accessories.

We offer products for:

- Preschool students
- Elementary school students
- Middle school students
- High school students
- College and university students
- Teachers
- Offices
- Art and creative projects

Our catalog is designed to include essential everyday products as well as specialized supplies for studying, organization, drawing, writing, and classroom activities.
